Good to know

Questions, answered.

How does delivery work?

Orders in Accra arrive within 48 hours, and free over GHS 400. We deliver across Ghana within 3 to 5 days.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Pay by Mobile Money or cash when your order arrives, so there is no risk.

Can I return or swap a pair?

Within 30 days, unworn and in the case. We'll swap it or refund you, no stress.

Do you make prescription lenses?

Our blue-light frames can be fitted with prescription lenses through our partner opticians. Ask us on WhatsApp.
